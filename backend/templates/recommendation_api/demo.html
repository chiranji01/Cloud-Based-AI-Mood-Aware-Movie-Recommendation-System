🤖 How does MoodFlix work?

MoodFlix uses a content-based recommendation approach. The selected mood is mapped to suitable genres and keywords. TF-IDF converts the movie features into numerical vectors, and cosine similarity measures how closely each movie matches the selected mood. Movie rating information and rating reliability are also considered to calculate the final ranking score and produce the top recommendations.
